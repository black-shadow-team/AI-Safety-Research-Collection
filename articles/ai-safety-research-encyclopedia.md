## Overview

This repository entry indexes the broad AI safety encyclopedia article published on AI in Plain English. It is intended as a starting point for defensive research across model security, alignment, governance, adversarial robustness, and responsible AI operation.

- Privacy-preserving AI

- Ethical AI governance

Security testing should be performed only against systems you own or where you have explicit written authorization. Defensive research must follow responsible disclosure and applicable law.
